The source strategy describes the European Commission's Cloud Sovereignty Framework as a way to evaluate cloud offerings using Sovereignty Effectiveness Assurance Levels (SEAL) and a weighted sovereignty score. The framework is relevant to customers assessing legal, operational, and technological control over their infrastructure.

This page captures the strategy's working interpretation, not legal or procurement advice. Verify the current framework, terminology, scores, and tender requirements against the [Commission's implementation guidance](https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/2ad80a48-166f-4c77-a513-80c53ca2a128_en?filename=Cloud%20Sovereignty%20Framework%20-%20Implementation%20guidance.pdf) before making compliance or procurement claims.

## SEAL levels

The strategy describes five levels, from SEAL-0 to SEAL-4, with the overall rating constrained by the lowest rating across the objectives:

| Level  | Working description                                                                                 |
| :----- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SEAL‑0 | No specific sovereignty safeguards.                                                                 |
| SEAL‑1 | Jurisdictional safeguards, including EU/EEA data hosting and GDPR compliance.                       |
| SEAL‑2 | Additional data safeguards, such as customer-managed encryption keys and audited access controls.   |
| SEAL‑3 | Technological autonomy, EU-controlled operations, and portable technology.                          |
| SEAL‑4 | Full end-to-end sovereignty, including the supply chain; described as aspirational in the strategy. |

## Evaluation criteria

The source identifies eight weighted categories for a global score. Their names, weights, and interpretation should be checked against the official guidance:

| Category                                    | Weight in source | Focus in source                                            |
| :------------------------------------------ | :--------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------- |
| SOV-1: Strategic sovereignty                | 15%              | Ownership, governance, financing, and resilience.          |
| SOV-2: Legal and jurisdictional sovereignty | 10%              | Exposure to foreign jurisdiction and compelled disclosure. |
| SOV-3: Data and AI sovereignty              | 10%              | Key control, access auditability, and processing location. |
| SOV-4: Operational sovereignty              | 15%              | Independent operation and maintenance.                     |
| SOV-5: Supply chain sovereignty             | 20%              | Hardware and firmware origins and supplier transparency.   |
| SOV-6: Technology sovereignty               | 15%              | Open standards, interoperability, and portability.         |
| SOV-7: Security and compliance              | 10%              | Security and regulatory requirements.                      |
| SOV-8: Environmental sustainability         | 5%               | Energy efficiency, emissions, and renewable power.         |

## Procurement and providers

The strategy describes a two-stage procurement model: the [Cloud III Dynamic Purchasing System](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/commission-launches-new-procurement-process-cloud-services-2024-02-07_en) pre-qualifies vendors, and subsequent competitions establish contracts for specific needs.

It reports a €180 million, six-year sovereign cloud tender awarded in April 2026 to Post Telecom with OVHcloud and CleverCloud, STACKIT, Scaleway, and Proximus with partners including S3NS, Clarence, and Mistral. The source reports SEAL-3 for the Post Telecom, STACKIT, and Scaleway offers and SEAL-2 for the Proximus offer. These are claims about particular tender offers, not a blanket certification of every service a provider sells.

Consult the [Commission's procurement announcement](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/commission-advances-cloud-sovereignty-through-strategic-procurement-2026-04-17_en), [press release](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/api/files/document/print/en/ip_26_833/IP_26_833_EN.pdf), and [framework explainer](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/sovereign-cloud-framework-explained-2026-06-01_en) for the applicable details.
